Product

One place for the work your course actually asks of you.

Course sites list everything. Skillmatch starts from who you are as a student and keeps the next resource close to the skills you are building.

01

A profile that drives the matches

Name, course, year of study, and interests. Those details are what the recommendations use. Empty interests mean fewer matches, so the profile is worth filling in.

02

Skills with a reason

Each suggestion is scored against your words, your course, and a difficulty that fits your year. You can see why a skill appeared instead of trusting a black box.

03

Resources you can act on

Courses, tutorials, videos, documentation, books, and projects, with platform and level. Open one, or save it, start it, and mark it complete.

04

Progress that stays yours

Saved, in progress, and completed live on the dashboard. A rating is optional. Nothing here is a public feed.

How it works

Four steps from a blank account to a path you can follow.

  1. 01

    Create your account

    Register with a username and password, then sign in. The student profile is created with your account.

  2. 02

    Describe what you study

    Add your course, year, and interests. Short phrases are enough. The matcher treats them as the brief for your recommendations.

  3. 03

    Open a matched resource

    Review the skill list and the resources attached to it. Links go to the original course, tutorial, or documentation.

  4. 04

    Track what you finish

    Save a resource, mark it in progress, or complete it. The dashboard counts each state so you can see movement, not just a list.

Why Skillmatch

Recommendations you can check, and a tracker that does not reset.

The matcher is built for students who need a next step, not another unsorted reading list. Sign in to use the same dashboard the recommendations feed.

  • Matched, not shuffled

    Suggestions come from your interests, course, and year. A first-year brief and a later-year brief should not receive the same difficulty.

  • Visible reasons

    Match scores and the terms that contributed are part of the product, so you can tell a strong fit from a weak one.

  • Your account, your progress

    Saved, started, and completed resources stay with the student who marked them. Sign in returns you to that dashboard.
